Privacy

How we handle information.

This notice describes the information handled through this website and when you contact us. It was last updated 4 November 2026.

Who is responsible

Triple X Dynamic Services is the business responsible for the services described on this site. ATYOM Studio is a brand operated by Triple X Dynamic Services and is the customer-facing name used to present those services. For privacy questions, contact support@atyom-studio.tech.

Business location: Abuja, Nigeria.

Information we handle

This site does not currently provide a contact form, account sign-in, or purchase flow. If you email us, we receive the information in your message and the email address and other details included with it. We use that information to respond to your enquiry and, if you ask us to, discuss or provide our services.

The website loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. A request to Google’s font services can disclose technical information such as your IP address, browser/device details and the page making the request so the fonts can be delivered. See Google Fonts privacy information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Service providers

Our support email is hosted using Titan Mail. Email providers process message and account data to provide their service; their own terms and privacy information apply to that processing. We do not currently use website analytics or advertising cookies in the code on this site. PostHog is planned for possible future use but is not currently installed. We will update this notice if our website data practices change.

Purpose, legal basis and retention

We use enquiry information to reply, take steps requested before a possible service agreement, and provide services you ask us to provide. The legal basis applicable to a particular enquiry depends on its context and may include steps at your request before entering a contract, performance of a contract, or consent where consent is the appropriate basis. We keep enquiry information only for as long as needed to handle the enquiry or service relationship and any applicable legal or recordkeeping requirements.

Your choices and rights

You can contact us at support@atyom-studio.tech to ask about personal information we hold about you or to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection where available under applicable law. If processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent. You may also raise a concern with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Rights and exceptions depend on applicable law and the circumstances.

Cookies and updates

The current site code does not set analytics or advertising cookies or use browser storage. Your browser or infrastructure provider may process technical request logs when the site is visited. If we add analytics, cookies, forms or other data collection, we will update this notice to describe the change before relying on it.

This notice may be revised as our services or website change. The date above indicates the latest update.
